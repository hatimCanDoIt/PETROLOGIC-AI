## Rw and Archie Sw

- RT is used in Archie to compute Sw; Rw is formation water resistivity (minimum
  resistivity of water in the rock / most water-wet baseline).
- Rw should be obtainable from the same log (minimum Rwa-style value), not
  require manual user input.
- Archie parameters (a, m, n) may be adjusted to obtain optimal Sw.

## Depth / play context

- Shallow reservoirs are uncommon in current work; expect pay intervals at
  **≥ ~10,000 ft** MD (deep targets).

## Cap rock (seal)

- A valid reservoir interval must have adequate **cap rock above** (seal).
  Open pay with no seal above is not a sound interpretation (migration risk).

## Porosity logs vs resistivity alone

- **Neutron and density porosity** (NPHI, DPHI) are primary HC indicators —
  not resistivity alone.
- Pay can show **NPHI–DPHI crossover or near-crossover** even when deep RT is
  only modest vs the well average; do not reject on RT alone.
- Corroborate with: low Vsh (GR), sandstone deflection on SP, high effective
  porosity.

## Multiple resistivity curves

- Compare **deep, medium, and shallow** resistivity for invasion / repeatability.
- If only deep RT is muted but medium/shallow or porosity logs show HC
  signature, investigate pay (invasion can depress deep RT).
